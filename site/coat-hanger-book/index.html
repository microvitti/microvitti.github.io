coat hanger book

elogio all’oggetto banale

estetizzazione di una gruccia. a book about the wire coat hanger, the most boring object in the house, and the artists who couldn’t stop looking at it: man ray, jasper johns, masao yamamoto. half essay, half collection of images, it follows the hanger from the closet to the museum.
