about me

vittoria scalmati is a furniture designer, editorial designer and artist, also working under the name microvitti. she is italian and lives and works in madrid. she studied fine arts and art history, then completed a master in furniture design at the politecnico di milano and worked in the studio of francesco faccin.

her work starts from the experience of being, or feeling, defenseless: the fear of not knowing how to protect herself and the fear of pain. it draws on memories, dreams, foggy things that happened and things that haven’t happened yet. her memory has always been unreliable and her mind is often clouded, so when she feels she is floating too much she holds on to what is concrete. she looks for materials that conduct temperature well, rough materials, anything that stimulates touch and keeps her feet on the ground.

what she looks for in everything she makes is that you want to touch it and aren’t afraid it will break. sometimes they still break. she hopes that one day all her objects will be strong like that. the stronger an object looks, the more it tends to come from a fragile and painful memory, and she isn’t sure whether the strength protects that memory or only covers it.
