museo

photo book

a small museum of ordinary objects: a pencil sharpener, a bell, a pair of dice, a few pencils. each one photographed in black and white as if it were a work of art, one object per page and a lot of silence around it. every photograph in the book is analog, shot on film: it took a very, very long time.
